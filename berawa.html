Berawa Beach · Bali

Bring Berawa
back to life

A week ago, a major fire destroyed many of the bars and businesses along Berawa Beach.

But the beach is still here. And we believe the best way to support it is to bring people back — to gather, move, create, surf, skim, eat, share and celebrate together.

On September 12–13, we are bringing the Berawa Skimboarding Festival & Competition back to the beach.

And we want to invite local small businesses to join us.

Bring your umbrella, products, food, drinks, clothes, art or anything you’d like to share.

Set up your spot on the beach and become part of the weekend.

You don’t need to be a sponsor. You don’t need a big budget.

Just show up, bring your vibe and help us bring Berawa back to life.

If you want to join us, send us a DM.
