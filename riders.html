We’re building skimboarding in Indonesia from scratch — and this September we’re throwing the first international gathering for it. Grab your board and come skim with us.

It’s a gathering, not a war. Teaching, sharing, sunset sessions — riders from across Asia on one beach.

The wave

A proper
shore break

Berawa sits right in the middle of Canggu — a shore break that fires metres from dry sand, warm water all year, waves that shift through the day.

No lineup, no paddle battle — just the last ten metres, over and over.

Who’s behind it

MIK
Skimboards

A board shop and workshop in Canggu, and Bali’s first skim school. We shape boards, teach the sport, and ride it — an active part of the global skim community.
